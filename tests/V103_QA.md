# ZoneRPG v10.3 QA

## Combat
`ensureEventActions` provides an executable exit only when every authored choice is unavailable or already claimed. Orphaned combat event saves recover to their result, and invalid continuations no longer trap the player. Missing generic post-combat definitions have a minimal result fallback. Standalone expedition victories keep expedition origin. Existing authored actions and death/retreat logic remain.

## Quiet Well
Quest `village_medic` requires `worldFlags.quietWellMedicalCrateFound`, set only by `v103_medical_crate` -> `Осмотреть аптечный ящик` in deadVillage with the quest active. Generic objective progress explicitly excludes this quest. Location and quest journal offer `Искать аптечный ящик`. Migration clears unsupported old active progress; historical completed quests and rewards stay intact.

## Shops
`shops.js` defines category chances, trader pools and item overrides. Stock is generated deterministically from saved world seed, game day, trader and item. Each trader's record is stored in `traderStocks`. Midnight refreshes all records; rendering, navigation and load do not reroll. Counts decrease after successful atomic purchases. Schema 16 migrates versions 2–15 while preserving dual-save keys and legacy copy behavior.

Common water/food have 96% availability; common supplies 94%; ammunition 82%; uncommon 60%; rare 22%; very rare 6%. Specialist technician/laboratory/faction pools retain their equipment. Unique goods have quantity one. Basic medical kits have 1–3 units, food 2–6, water 3–8, ammo 18–60 rounds. Prices still use existing faction/world modifiers.

Stackable goods have a quantity slider and immediate total. Maximum respects money, available stock and any existing stack limit. Engine separately validates quantity, money, stock, unique items and weight before charging. Depleted goods display `Нет в наличии`. Non-stackable items have no slider.

## Mutants
Mutants cannot offer weapon inspection or weapon/ammo/parts drops. Biological examination grants local `mutantParts` with an original SVG specimen icon. Human weapon inspection remains. The engine also guards mutant salvage outcomes.

## UI and balance
Shop/inventory rows use smaller padding, gaps and icons. Stack controls are compact and wrap below 400px. Weapon summaries share definitions for damage and ammunition; melee explicitly needs no ammo. `ZoneRPGFormat.percent` rounds to at most one decimal and is reused in location, map, stat and combat output. AKS-74U damage changed 28 -> 35 (+25%); AKM remains 37, accuracy, fire rate, magazine and price unchanged.
